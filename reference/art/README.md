# Rider–Waite–Smith card art

Local snapshots, up to 600 px wide, of the complete 78-card Rider–Waite–Smith
deck used by Cards & Decks Lab for Open Table and Celtic Cross recipes. Keeping
the art beside the reading data avoids runtime image redirects.

The illustrations are Pamela Colman Smith's work for A. E. Waite's *The
Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1910) and are in the public domain. Files were
sourced from the corresponding `Pictorial Key to the Tarot …` images in the
[Wikimedia Commons collection](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:The_Pictorial_Key_to_the_Tarot)
on 2026-08-09. Wikimedia Commons is the distribution source, not the rights
holder.

Filenames use `major-00.jpg` through `major-21.jpg` and
`<suit>-01.jpg` through `<suit>-14.jpg` for wands, cups, swords, and
pentacles.
